Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FiveMDepot.

FiveMDepot is a premium marketplace for FiveM server assets. We connect developers who create scripts, MLOs, maps, and vehicles with FiveM server owners looking to enhance their roleplay experience. Every asset is reviewed for quality before being listed.
Browse our marketplace, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. We support bKash, Nagad, and bank transfer payments. After you submit your payment proof, our admin team reviews and approves it, then unlocks your secure download links.
We offer three main categories: Scripts (ESX & QBCore compatible frameworks, jobs, UI packs), MLOs & Maps (custom interiors, buildings, map edits), and Vehicles (custom cars with unique handling and liveries). New assets are added regularly.
Create an account and submit a seller application. Our team reviews each application to ensure quality. Once approved, you can start listing your FiveM assets, track your earnings, and manage your storefront from your seller dashboard.
We currently support bKash, Nagad, and bank transfer payments. These methods are chosen to serve our community in South Asia and beyond. Payment instructions are displayed at checkout. Admin reviews each payment proof for security.
Due to the digital nature of our products, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. If you experience technical issues with a purchased asset, contact our support team and we'll work to resolve the issue or arrange a refund if appropriate.
