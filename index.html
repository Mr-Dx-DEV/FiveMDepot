FAQs

What is FiveMDepot?

FiveMDepot is an online marketplace for FiveM server assets, offering scripts, maps, MLOs, and vehicles.

Simply browse our categories, select your desired items, and proceed to checkout.

What types of assets are available?

We provide a wide range of assets including scripts, MLOs, maps, and vehicles from various creators.

After your purchase, you will receive instant access to your downloaded files in your account.

Yes, our support team is ready to assist you with any questions or issues.

Absolutely! We welcome suggestions for new assets to add to our marketplace.
