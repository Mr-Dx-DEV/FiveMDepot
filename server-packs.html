    FAQ

    Server pack questions

    Do I need coding experience?
    No. Packs come pre-configured. You only change settings like your server name and prices in config files, and our setup guide covers every step.
    What hosting do I need?
    A VPS or dedicated server with at least 4 GB RAM (8 GB+ recommended for 64+ players), plus a MySQL/MariaDB database. Any FiveM-ready host works.
    Are updates really free?
    Yes. Every update to your pack is added to your library at no extra cost, for as long as the pack is maintained.
    Can I add my own scripts?
    Absolutely. The packs use a standard QBCore structure, so any compatible resource from the store (or your own) can be added.
    Can I resell or share the pack?
    No. Your licence covers servers you own or manage. See our Terms of Service.
    What if it doesn’t work?
    Open a ticket on Discord — we help you get it running. If a problem can’t be fixed, our Refund Policy applies.
